**Signal admission and trade-management investigation — 23 September 2026**

The system uses setup and market intelligence, but it does not use one consistent decision snapshot throughout the workflow. Confirmed defects allow grading, later filtering, and execution metadata to disagree. The exit stack also contains a concrete option-price comparison bug and several policies that turn 5-minute/15-minute setups into short scalps. The evidence supports repairing consistency before changing thresholds. It does not establish that removing filters or holding every trade longer would improve results.

The effective workflow is:

`Raw engine arrows → own-timeframe grade → candidate selection → routing and preliminary gates → intrabar-only buffer/regrade → final quality/data/location checks → contract geometry and execution checks → trade management → first eligible exit.`

**How the grade and filters actually work.** The grade is a display bucket of a heuristic confidence score: A+ starts at 85%, A at 70%, B+ at 55%, B at 40%. The score combines technical evidence (ADX, multi-timeframe confluence, stop distance, rejection wicks), direction-aligned intelligence, regime alignment, VIX, and later adjustments/caps. The intelligence aggregator incorporates measured volume, OI/PCR, options context, regime, candle pressure, divergence, and session/breadth context. Thus intelligence is used; the problem is how its outputs are combined and passed between decisions. A displayed 96% is **not a measured 96% probability of a profitable trade**, because live calibration is disabled. [Grade bands](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_quality_policy.py:39), [scoring](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_manager.py:266), [aggregation](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/intelligence/intelligence_aggregator.py:473).

| Ordinary quality route | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Normal known regime | B+ or better; measured ADX at least 22; two confirmation legs below 70% confidence, one at/above 70% |
| Risky/ranging regime | B+ or better; ADX at least 25; 15m alignment plus another confirmation |
| UNKNOWN regime | At least 70%; ADX at least 25; 15m alignment and three total confirmation legs |
| Confirmed market shift | Explicit alternative route, with its own confidence and measured-volume conditions |
| Full-core 5m market shift | Separate exception can admit below B+ with at least 35% confidence, ADX 18, and three synchronized core votes |

Confirmation legs are 15m alignment, impulse/power, measured volume, and multiple signal sources. A pass is not an order: price action, session direction, location, data freshness, availability, portfolio limits, quotes, risk geometry and execution checks can still veto it. Conversely, explicit exceptions can bypass some ordinary checks. The selected grade preference of B does not remove the canonical B+ floor for ordinary entries. Expected-move magnitude is advisory in the final entry policy, rather than the old hard forecast-size veto. [Quality policy](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/quality_verdict.py:295), [forecast advisory](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/entry_policy.py:783).

**1. High priority: option profit locks test the wrong price.** The live loop translates a premium profit floor into an index level and tests the index against that level. That is not equivalent to testing the verified option premium against its premium floor. Premium and index movement need not follow the fixed entry-delta conversion.

A reproducible synthetic case uses an option bought at ₹100, verified premium ₹116, quantity 65, and observed peak profit ₹1,040. The calculated premium lock is ₹110.432. The premium has not breached it. With an unchanged index at 25,000, the translated index stop is 25,020.864, so the code nevertheless requests `SMART_PROFIT_LOCK` at ₹110.432. The probe exercises the actual arming, breathing-room, trend-deferral, and close-wrapper checks; a 30-second-old 15m ranging/scalp trade passes them. Unrelated earlier exits are assumed idle.

This can create premature option exits and misleading requested/simulated exit prices. Actual broker fills are separate. The reproduction proves a mechanism, not that a particular recorded loss was caused by it. The correct decision must distinguish an underlying structural stop from a premium profit lock and test each against its own observed price. [Comparison](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/scanner.py:36887), [exit request](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/scanner.py:36926), [reproduction](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/reports/workflow_exit_probes_20260923.json).

**2. High priority: grading and routing/filtering can use different timeframes.** The processor grades the selected signal with its own-timeframe intelligence. Later instrument routing and the FUT direction veto still use the caller's 5m regime/intelligence. Metadata is corrected to the graded timeframe only afterward.

Executing the actual veto expression with a 15m BUY, 15m TRENDING_UP and 5m TRENDING_DOWN produces a FUT veto; applying the own-timeframe context produces no veto. Reversing those regimes misses the veto that the own-timeframe context requires. AUTO routing may convert the false FUT veto into an option route. This establishes inconsistent context, not that any wrongly unvetoed candidate necessarily survives the remaining gates. If 5m context is intentionally a separate execution filter, it must be represented and validated as such instead of silently replacing the context that produced the grade. [Own-timeframe grading](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_processor.py:6067), [routing](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_processor.py:7012), [veto](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_processor.py:7034), [probe results](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/reports/workflow_entry_probes_20260923.json).

**3. High priority in intrabar mode: buffer regrading is mathematically inconsistent.** Maturity recalculates intelligence/regime contributions but adds the old adjustment as a constant. That adjustment includes nonlinear operations such as an exhaustion cap. A cap must be reapplied to the new score; its previous subtraction cannot be reused.

With ADX 45, direct current grading produces B+ at 59.27%, while buffered regrading produces B at 44.00%: a candidate can fall below the 55% floor solely because of the inconsistent calculation. In the reverse update, direct grading caps confidence at 82%, but buffered grading produces A+ at 97.27%. These are controlled examples, not estimates of the frequency of missed trades. This defect is dormant for new ordinary candidates under the current saved CANDLE_CLOSE mode. [Faulty recombination](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/scanner.py:20633), [original cap](/C:/Users/sagnik/Desktop/rp46-3/engine/signal_manager.py:392).

**5. The scalp behavior is supported by both policy and recent fills.** Among the latest filled LIVE trades in the September 15–22 audit window, the database contains ten fills, all on September 16–18. Nine lost money and one gained; total app-recorded P&L was −₹3,069. Median fill-to-exit duration was 130.7 seconds; six exited within three minutes; nine carried `scalp_lock_mode`. Exit reasons were four BREAKEVEN_STOP, three REPAINT_ABORT, two EARLY_LOSS_CUT and one SMART_PROFIT_LOCK. A 15m NIFTY trade exited after 27.8 seconds through SMART_PROFIT_LOCK. Margin-preflight rejection and historical/backtest rows were excluded. Actual broker costs/reconciliation fields were not populated, so this is the app's recorded result.

This behavior has direct code support. RANGING/UNKNOWN can trail the previous **one-minute** candle extreme, irrespective of the source setup timeframe. Scalp/choppy profit locks can bypass the usual timeframe maturation check after a peak threshold. Early loss protection starts after a configured 15-second minimum; breakeven protection has a 60-second minimum and a 0.4R arming component. R is the trade's initial monetary risk. These are eligibility conditions, not guaranteed exit times. Runner mode can start at 0.4R and deliberately permits missing ADX; that explains recorded runner descriptions with `adx=0.0` without proving an ADX parsing bug. There is also a reproduced scanner/trade-manager disagreement: scanner early-loss logic can defer, then the manager's R-based backstop can cut. The narrow-risk example's admission through current entry policy is unproven, and earlier profit exits may mask the green-trade example. A reliable funnel must preserve one lifecycle identity with ordered gate decisions and an explicit distinction between missing data, weak setup, portfolio refusal and execution failure. Existing shadow evidence is useful, but it does not yet attribute the incremental benefit or harm of each individual filter.

**Recommended repair sequence.** First correct the option profit-lock price comparison. Then use one versioned decision snapshot for grading, routing, final quality and entry metadata, with explicit timeframe and observation time; refresh 15m confirmation at admission. For intrabar mode, run the same full grading function at maturity and refresh machine-readable evidence flags instead of treating old prose as authorization.

Next define a trade-management policy at entry for each intended setup horizon. One evaluator should resolve discretionary exit precedence and emit a structured HOLD/TIGHTEN/EXIT decision; execution should enforce hard risk/market safety and carry out that decision. A 15m setup should enter a scalp policy only through an explicit recorded choice. A runner/hold policy must have a defined relationship to 1m trailing, profit locks, breakeven, and loss backstops. Do not infer that deleting these protections is safe from the short duration alone.

Finally compare identical timestamped raw events and exact option contracts through the current gates and one-at-a-time filter removals, with causal data availability, bid/ask, realistic costs, overlapping-position constraints and fixed evaluation splits. Separately compare exits on identical accepted entries. That separates entry selection, delayed execution and premature exits instead of optimizing all three against the same small sample.
